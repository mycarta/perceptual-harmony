Perceptual Harmony

Check whether the colours of a palette are harmonious, measured in a perceptual colour space. Fits the hue templates of Cohen-Or et al. (2006), checks each verdict in four colour spaces, simulates colour-vision deficiency, and suggests new colours as CSS oklch().

Runs entirely in your browser: the Python package perceptual-harmony, loaded with Pyodide. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  
Hue templates from Cohen-Or, Sorkine, Gal, Leyvand & Xu (2006), Color harmonization, after Matsuda. Colour-vision
  deficiency simulated as in Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009). Results are information for the
  designer, not rules.
